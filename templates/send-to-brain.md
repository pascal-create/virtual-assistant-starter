# Topic in a few words

Source: who or what it came from (Grok and the bot's name, a meeting, an email, a chat, a voice memo), plus a link if there is one
Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Area: which business, or "personal"

## Summary
2 to 5 lines.

## Decisions
- What was decided, by whom, and why.

## Commitments
- Who will do what, by YYYY-MM-DD (a real date).

## People and companies
- Name, role, company, email or phone if given. Source.

## Facts to update
- What changed (a price, a status, the team, a goal), the old value if known, the source.

## Raw
The original words, pasted as-is.
