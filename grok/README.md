# Grok

Your Grok Chief of Staff is the assistant you talk to from your phone. Claude (in VS Code) is the office manager who files. These files set up Grok so it reads the same brain.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `chief-of-staff.template.md` | The blank Chief of Staff instructions. Don't edit it. |
| `inbox-manager.template.md` | The blank Inbox Manager profile, your first specialist. Don't edit it. |
| `prompts.md` | The blank prompts, C2 to C9, and the 6-part bot recipe. |
| `my-chief-of-staff.md` | Your filled-in instructions. The setup makes it. |
| `my-prompts.md` | Your filled-in prompts. The setup makes it. |
| `my-inbox-manager.md` | Your filled-in Inbox Manager profile. Say "fill my Inbox Manager" to make it. |

## How to use them
1. **Lock the door first.** In Grok Bot, click your name (bottom left) > Settings > General > Data sharing, and turn off "Improve Grok Bot and more". One switch covers Grok Bot, Grok and Cursor. If you also use X, untick Grok data sharing in X (Settings > Privacy and safety > Grok).
2. In Claude Code, say **"copy my Grok instructions"**. They land on your clipboard.
3. In Grok, create a new bot, give it its name and pin it. Paste the instructions as your first message. It answers with its understanding in 5 lines.
4. Connect Google Drive in Grok, with the same Google account as your brain folder. Then say "copy Grok prompt C2" in Claude Code and paste it to your bot.
5. Next: C3, your morning brief.

## Good to know
- The safety rules are already inside the instructions: drafts first, never delete, never type a password, no spending without a yes.
- Grok writes only into `inbox/`. Claude files everything else.
- The Drive connection can likely see your whole Drive, not just this folder. The instructions limit what Grok does, not what it can reach. Keep sensitive files out of that Google account, or use a separate account for your assistant.
- The menu names in Grok can change. If a step looks different, look for the closest match.
