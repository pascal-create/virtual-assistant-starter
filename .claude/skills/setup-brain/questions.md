# Setup questions

Ask one at a time, in this order. Each question comes with a short example. The examples all use the same person, so the answers feel real: **Julie, owner of a 6-person landscaping company in Halifax.** Never save the example as their answer.

End every question with "(say skip to skip)".

At the first question of each round, start with the round line, for example: "Round 2 of 7: Your work (about 5 minutes)".

Where it says **Follow-up**, that's the one follow-up you may ask if the answer is under about 5 words or misses the specifics.

---

## Step 0: what you already have

> Do you already have documents about you or your business (a bio, an "about us", a price list, a brand doc, a profile from ChatGPT)? Drag them into this chat or into the inbox folder, or paste them. Say "none" to skip.
>
> Tip: in ChatGPT, ask "Write out everything you know about me: my role, my business, how I work and how I communicate, so I can paste it into another AI." and paste the answer here.

---

## Round 1 of 7: Hello (about 2 minutes)

**Q1. Name**
> What should I call you, and what's your full name?
> Example: "Julie. My full name is Julie Martin."
> (say skip to skip)

**Q2. Languages**
> Which languages do you work in, and with whom?
> Example: "English with most clients. French with my crew and my family. I write my emails in English."
> (say skip to skip)

Save the language they write in as `language` in `progress.md`.

---

## Round 2 of 7: Your work (about 5 minutes)

**Q3. What you do**
> What do you do, in one or two sentences? If you have a website, paste the link too.
> Example: "I run a landscaping company in Halifax with 6 people. We do lawn care, patios and snow removal for homeowners and a few small businesses."
> (say skip to skip)

Follow-up: "Who do you do it for, and where?"

**Q4. What you sell**
> What do you sell, and to whom? Rough prices help, but you can skip them.
> Example: "Weekly lawn care for homeowners, about $45 a visit. Patios, usually $8,000 to $20,000. Snow removal contracts for small businesses, by the season."
> (say skip to skip)

If they skip the prices, don't ask again. Prices become a gap.

**Q5. Your team**
> Who works with you? Names and roles. Include part-timers, contractors, a bookkeeper or a business partner.
> Example: "Luc is my foreman. Sarah does the books two days a week. I have 4 seasonal crew members."
> (say skip to skip)

**Q6. Your tools**
> Which tools run your day? Email, calendar, phone, apps, software.
> Example: "Gmail, Google Calendar, my phone, Jobber for quotes, QuickBooks, and Facebook Messenger with clients."
> (say skip to skip)

---

## Round 3 of 7: Right now (about 4 minutes)

**Q7. 12 months from now**
> Picture yourself 12 months from now, and it went well. What's different?
> Example: "We do $900K in sales, I'm off the tools, Luc runs the crews, and I take Fridays off with my kids."
> (say skip to skip)

**Q8. Top 3 this month**
> What are your top 3 priorities this month?
> Example: "1. Hire two crew members for spring. 2. Send the snow removal invoices. 3. Get 10 new Google reviews."
> (say skip to skip)

**Q9. What eats your time**
> What eats your hours every week that you'd hand off tomorrow if you could? Roughly how many hours a week is that?
> Example: "Answering the same quote questions, chasing invoices and building the crew schedule. About 8 hours a week."
> (say skip to skip)

Follow-up, only if there's no number: "Roughly how many hours a week, all together?"
After Q9: the optional Calendar boost (see SKILL.md).

---

## Round 4 of 7: How to work with you (about 3 minutes)

**Q10. How to answer you**
> How should I answer you? Think about length, tone, and how direct you want me to be.
> Example: "Short. Give me the answer first, then the why. Tell me straight if my idea is bad."
> (say skip to skip)

**Q11. Never do**
> What should an assistant never do for you?
> Example: "Never send an email without showing me first. Never book anything on Sundays. No emojis with clients."
> (say skip to skip)

**Q12. How you work best (private)**
> Is there anything about how you work best that would help me help you? This stays private: I'll never put it in anything written for someone else.
> Example: "I get distracted easily, so one thing at a time. My best hours are 6 to 10 in the morning. After 4, I'm done."
> (say skip to skip)

Saved as Private by default. If they say part of it can be shared, note which part.

---

## Round 5 of 7: Your people (about 4 minutes)

**Q13. One real customer**
> Think of one real customer you liked working with. No name needed. Who were they, and what was going on for them when they came to you?
> Example: "A couple in their 60s. They had just bought a house with a yard full of weeds, and their daughter's wedding was happening there in 3 months."
> (say skip to skip)

**Q14. Their words**
> When people first contact you, what do they say? Use their words if you remember them.
> Example: "'My yard is a disaster and I don't know where to start.' 'Can you come before the long weekend?'"
> (say skip to skip)

**Q15. What makes them hesitate**
> What makes people hesitate before they buy from you?
> Example: "Price. Someone with a truck quoted them less. And they worry we won't show up when we say."
> (say skip to skip)

Before Round 6: the optional Gmail boost (see SKILL.md).

---

## Round 6 of 7: Your voice (about 6 minutes)

**Q16. Tell a friend**
> Tell me what you do like you're telling a friend over coffee. Use dictation and just ramble for a minute. Don't clean it up.
> Example: "So basically, we make yards look good. Most people call me when it's already a mess, and honestly, that's my favourite part..."
> (say skip to skip)

**Q17. Your words**
> Which words or phrases do you use all the time? And which words would you never say?
> Example: "I say 'no worries' and 'let's get it done' a lot. I'd never say 'synergy' or 'I hope this email finds you well'."
> (say skip to skip)

**Q18. A message that sounds like you**
> Paste one email or message you wrote that sounds like you. Take out anything private first, like phone numbers or addresses.
> Example: "Hi Dave, thanks for the pics. We can start Tuesday at 8. I'll bring the sod. Talk soon, Julie"
> (say skip to skip)

---

## Round 7 of 7: Your Grok team (about 2 minutes)

**Q19. Your Chief of Staff's name**
> Last round! Your Grok Chief of Staff is the assistant you'll talk to from your phone. What name do you want to give it?
> Example: "Sam."
> (say skip to skip)

If skipped, the name is "Chief of Staff".

**Q20. Morning brief**
> What time should it send your morning brief each weekday, and what's your time zone?
> Example: "7:00 AM, Atlantic time. I'm in Halifax."
> (say skip to skip)

> Which Google account holds this folder? It's the address you see at the top right of Google Drive, like name@gmail.com.
> (say skip to skip)

Save it as `google_account` in `progress.md`.

Then: "That's all my questions. Say 'I'm done' and I'll build your brain."
